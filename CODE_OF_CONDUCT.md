# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, especially to beginners — they are who Tacit is for. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers through [GitHub](https://github.com/DiogoKaster) or the [He4rt Developers](https://github.com/he4rt) organization. Reports are handled privately.
